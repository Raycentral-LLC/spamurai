Spamurai Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026. Spamurai is published by RayCentral LLC.

Summary

Spamurai does not collect, store or share any personal data. There is no account, no analytics and no advertising SDK in the app.

Safari content blocking

Safari applies Spamurai's block lists itself. Spamurai never sees the pages you visit.

System-wide blocking (Pro)

Spamurai Pro uses a local VPN configuration that handles only DNS lookups on your iPhone. Lookups for listed ad and tracker domains are answered with "not found". Other lookups are resolved over encrypted DNS (DNS-over-HTTPS) by Cloudflare (1.1.1.1), which, like any DNS resolver, receives the domain names being looked up; see Cloudflare's 1.1.1.1 privacy policy. The rest of your traffic does not pass through Spamurai.

The Activity journal and statistics are stored only on your device, inside the app, and are never transmitted. Deleting the app deletes them.

Filter updates

The app downloads updated filter lists from GitHub Pages. These are plain file downloads; GitHub may log your IP address as with any web request.

Purchases

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. Spamurai verifies them on the device and receives no payment details.

Contact

Questions: open an issue at github.com/Raycentral-LLC/spamurai.
